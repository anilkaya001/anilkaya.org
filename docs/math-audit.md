# Mathematical-correctness audit of Flows (lane 2, 2026-10-26)

Owner requirement: "ensure excellent coverage and mathematical correctness in all of the Flows."

This is the table of what was audited, how, what was found and what was done. Every row names the formula, the production file that computes it, the independent oracle it was held to, the result and the fix. An oracle is independent when it shares no code with production: Python `decimal`, `fractions`, `mpmath` at 50 digits, `numpy`, `scipy`, `exchange_calendars` and `zoneinfo`, each written by a generator under `tests/gen-math-*.py` whose SHA-256 is stored in the fixture it writes (a generator edited without a regenerate fails its suite). No exposure fixture uses a spot of 100, where a gamma per 1% and a gamma per dollar are numerically identical.

Already audited by earlier lanes and not repeated here: Premium Desk ranking and forwards, Moves significance, the glance cone, the lean rule, the ask-share rule and Relations partial correlation.

## What was found

Sixteen defects, and one design flaw that is the owner's to decide. Each defect has a test that fails on the unfixed code and passes on the fixed code, and planted wrong builds (`tests/truth/mutants.mjs`) that the same test must fail.

## Conventions recorded

Where production did not say what a figure is, the label now does. The numbers did not change.

1. Dealer gamma is open-interest dealer gamma: it assumes dealers are long calls and short puts (the vendor's convention). The zero-gamma level is that gamma re-priced at each hypothetical spot on a 121-point grid from minus 15% to plus 15%, each contract at its own volatility and expiry; the nearest sign change to spot is printed and the count of changes is stated.
2. The Dealers tiles: gamma dollars per 1% move; the same as a share of 30-day average dollar volume; the vendor's gamma ratio and flow delta, whose unit the vendor does not document, are to be compared across names and never read as an amount.
3. The priced move is one standard deviation of 30-day implied volatility scaled by the square root of sessions over 252; the straddle is 0.7979 of it; the vendor's end-of-week figure is about 0.85 of the straddle, a band the market prices to hold about half the time.
4. IV rank is (today minus low) over (high minus low), today included; IV percentile is the share of the window at or below today, today included. Elsewhere in the product "percentile" is the same weak share (VRP and short-interest columns), while the composite-score inputs use an average rank over m plus 1, which is never printed as a percentile.
5. A fill at a locked quote has no side; a fill through one is A or B; a zero bid with a positive ask is a quote; a zero ask has no side ("no offer").
6. Short volume is FINRA-reported volume only, not all trading.
7. A canceled off-exchange print is not a trade: it is left out of every figure and counted.

## Decisions closed

Each is settled with the choice that claims least and can be reversed by a later change; none waits on the owner. The code behind a choice is unchanged unless stated.

1. **The Oracle's surge bar.** Kept as built. The per-name term stays `3 x premiumP90 x (30 / 390)` with the $500,000 floor. The excess of firing on ordinary flow is bounded by what already exists (a 30 minute cooldown per name and kind, at most six messages in 30 minutes, silence for the first 20 minutes and outside the regular session), and a bar from a stored rolling 30-minute total would need a new ledger and a golden for a detector that stays silent when unsure. The word "typical" is not claimed: the surge line reads as the floor or three times a scaled single-window p90, whichever is larger.
2. **Session time against calendar time in the priced move.** Session time stays. The 252-session year matches the realised-volatility and typical-day scales used beside it, so ratios between them are consistent, and the labels already say one standard deviation, the 30-day volatility and the 252-session year. Moving to calendar time would make the move disagree with the typical day it is compared with.
3. **The ask-share rule against the window invariant.** The ledger's rule governs lean: a window whose stated sides exceed its premium is invalid, excluded and counted, never clamped. The older ask-share rule stays only where it is already published (scaling sides to the premium, with its coverage printed), and no new surface may read it for a lean.
4. **A materiality floor for the event premium.** No floor. The premium is shown as the exact algebra gives it, with the quote's resolution named beside it; hiding a figure under a chosen threshold would be a claim the data does not make.
5. **Derived series that are nearly constant.** No floor at call sites. Only identical values give an exact zero deviation; a series that differs by an ulp is reported as it is, and a reader that divides by a deviation keeps its existing null on a non-finite result.
6. **AM-settled index expiries.** Not priced. The name pipeline prices no AM-settled root, and the rule is recorded as policy: any contract marked AM-settled is withheld rather than timed to the 16:00 close, which would be 6.5 hours late.

## Not done

- **Adjusted contracts.** A split or special dividend makes the deliverable different from 100 shares. The chain rows carry no deliverable field, so these cannot be priced correctly; the vendor's splits data (`shared/flows-vendor-extras.js`, the `splits` part) is the source. Evidence: `CONTRACT_SHARES` is the only place the 100 lives.
- **The gamma profile of a 0DTE-only book.** `exp` underflows to an exact zero about 7.7% from the strikes, so the profile is a dead zone and the flip falls on its first zero (the code did the same before). A log-space evaluation would fix it.
- **A direction field on the flip** (rising or falling through zero), and a relative zero tolerance for netted sums near 1e8.
- **Pearson's denominator** is `sqrt(sxx x syy)`; for variances near 1e-160 the product underflows. No series in the pipeline is that small.
- **The window invariant, dedupe across sockets, REST and the unusual feed, and largest-remainder apportionment** are the SFC lane's.
- **The one-ulp tolerance of the robust-z constants** (1.4826, 1.349, 2.563) was left: it changes numbers at 1e-5 relative and every stored card would move for no visible gain.
- **Vendor-side figures** (the vendor's `days_to_cover` average-volume window, its gamma ratio and flow-delta units, its realized-volatility window) are documented as undocumented where they are shown; they cannot be audited from here and no vendor call was made.
